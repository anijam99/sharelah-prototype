## Reading decisions

- Repeated J-Prem 2-for-S$16.80 tags are one offer. The two beef signs repeat the same promotion; the three named 200 g ribeye variants have separate entries, all at the same 2-for-S$19.90 total.
- The Cold Storage screenshot contains six fully readable product cards plus a delivery banner. Lower-row yoghurt products are partly cut off with no readable prices and were not assigned invented prices.
- The clearance photo contains many individual products. Readable brands or product groups are listed separately; indistinct variants remain grouped. Percentage-only stickers have no assumed final price. Two legible handwritten prices are retained with the relevant photo.
- The detergent image has no price, pack size or offer. It is included as a clearly labelled product reference.
- The diffuser photograph also shows Lavender & Eucalyptus bottles without a legible price; this is noted in the Yuzu & Lime entry rather than assuming the same promotion.
- The bottom-left beer tag advertises 2 for S$7 but its product name is unclear and it is marked temporarily out of stock. It has a clearly marked entry with no split action.
- Other tiny background shelf tags and partly obscured prices were inspected but not promoted to named deals when the associated product or price could not be read.
- Advertised bundle prices are stored as bundle totals. Known multibuy quantities and one-item portions prefill the split form. Single-item, restaurant, percentage-only, card, voucher and uncertain offers do not prefill a priced split.

## Assets and persistence

Optimized JPEGs for all 12 sources are bundled in `public/assets/deals/` (about 4.4 MB combined), with individually framed thumbnails and detail images. Supplied originals remain in `some images of deals/`. Existing saved demos receive the catalogue without losing reservations, saved items, drafts or personal reports. Reset restores the bundled photo collection.

## Individual deal crops and main splits

`src/dealPhotos.ts` defines a separate product/sign crop for every entry, with a larger offer crop where useful. `Photo` clips the original pixels in the browser and contains the whole selected region inside each card, avoiding an additional centre crop that would cut off labels. Full source photos remain accessible from deal details. The crop reference travels with the photo into newly created splits.

The main sample splits use Chobani blueberry yoghurt (2 for S$7.30; S$3.80 per portion including the fee), Carefree liners (2 for S$3.90; S$2.10 including the fee), and Buldak noodles (2 for S$6.40; S$3.35 including the fee). Titles, photos, portions, categories, stores, and bundle prices come from the same catalogue entries. Pickup locations remain sample Bugis meeting points. Legacy IDs and collection codes are retained so existing reservations, reviews and chats survive refresh.

Crop inspection corrected the clearance shelf's Avène Cleanance sunscreen and Maybelline Fit Me names, removed an unverified brand from the evening primrose bottle, and separated Difflam from Strepsils.
